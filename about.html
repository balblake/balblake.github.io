Biography

I am an Electrical Engineering student at Texas A&M University minoring in Mathematics, with a 3.51 GPA and an expected graduation in May 2028. My coursework spans circuit theory, digital systems, machine learning, signals and systems, electronics, energy conversion, and electric and magnetic fields. I am especially interested in power systems and electrical design, while continuing to build broad experience across embedded and digital systems.

I like working where hardware and software meet. Through laboratory work and independent projects, I have built and validated analog circuits, implemented Verilog designs on an FPGA, and developed an ESP32-based RC vehicle with a custom browser controller and two-layer PCB. My data internship added experience using SQL, pandas, and Plotly to turn large datasets into clear engineering and business conclusions.

About Me

Beyond engineering, I have spent multiple seasons performing in the marching arts. I served as Bassline Section Leader with the Blue Stars Drum and Bugle Corps, performed with Pacific Crest, and taught percussion students as a Drumline Technician at Lewisville High School.

National tours across more than 50 cities demanded 12-hour rehearsal days, precise teamwork, and fast improvement under pressure. Leading four bassline members and co-leading 11 additional percussion performers strengthened the communication, resilience, and attention to detail I bring to engineering teams. I was honored with the Outstanding First Year Battery Member Award.
